When should I turn these off?

Turn off Auto-capture for one-off downloads that should stay in the browser; right-click actions and this popup still send links and pages to SwiftDM. Private or signed-in files (e.g. a Google Drive share) need Send browser cookies so the session travels with the request. Detected HLS playlists and video/audio pairs are sent as ordinary URLs; the app downloads the playlist segments itself.
